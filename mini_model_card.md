# Mini Model Card

## System Name

Hybrid Complaint Escalation Risk System

---

## Intended Use

The Hybrid Complaint Escalation Risk System is designed to assist e-commerce customer support teams in identifying customer complaints that may require escalation.

The system combines structured customer data, complaint text, product image information, and sequence-based text analysis to estimate escalation risk and recommend an appropriate next action.

The system is intended as a decision-support tool and not as an autonomous decision-making system.

Typical use cases include:

* Customer complaint triage
* Support ticket prioritization
* Escalation risk assessment
* Customer service workflow optimization

---

## Not Intended For

This system should not be used for:

* Automatic approval or rejection of refunds
* Legal decision-making
* Financial decision-making
* Medical decision-making
* Hiring or employment decisions
* Customer account suspension decisions
* Any fully automated customer resolution process

The system should not replace human judgment for medium-risk or high-risk complaints.

---

## Model Components

### Classical ML

* Model: Logistic Regression
* Feature Engineering: TF-IDF Vectorization
* Purpose: Complaint text classification baseline

### CNN

* Model: Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)
* Purpose: Product image classification
* Categories: Normal / Damaged

### RNN / LSTM

* Model: LSTM
* Purpose: Sequence-based complaint text analysis
* Learns contextual word patterns associated with escalation risk

### LLM

* Model: Gemini
* Purpose:

  * Complaint summarization
  * Risk explanation
  * Recommended action generation
  * Human review recommendation

The LLM does not modify model predictions.

### Tabular Inputs

* Customer tenure

* Order value

* Customer complaint text

Examples:

* "Delivery was slightly delayed"
* "The product arrived broken and nobody responded"

### Image Inputs

* Product image
* Product screenshot

Categories used in prototype:

* Normal
* Damaged

### Sequence Inputs

* Complaint text sequence processed through the LSTM model

### Risk Score

Continuous score between 0 and 1.

Example:

### Risk Level

* Low
* Medium
* High

### Recommended Action

* auto_reply
* manual_review
* escalate_immediately

### Explanation

Generated by the LLM and includes:

* Summary
* Reason
* Risk notes
* Human review recommendation

## Known Failure Modes

Potential failure scenarios include:

* Sarcastic complaint text
* Ambiguous customer language
* Poor image quality
* Blurry or incomplete screenshots
* Incorrect synthetic labels
* Class imbalance
* Model disagreement
* LLM hallucination
* Out-of-distribution complaints
* Previously unseen product categories

Examples:

```text
"Great service! I've only been ignored for three weeks."
```

Such complaints may be difficult for the models to interpret correctly.

---

## Human Review Rules

Human review is required when:

* Risk level is medium or high
* Model outputs disagree significantly
* Complaint text is unclear
* Image quality is poor
* Customer information is incomplete
* LLM returns human_review_required = true
* Any business-critical action is required

Final customer-facing decisions remain the responsibility of human support staff.

---

## Responsible AI Considerations

The Hybrid Complaint Escalation Risk System is intended to support human decision-making through explainable and auditable predictions.

The system combines Classical ML, CNN, LSTM, and LLM components while maintaining human oversight for medium-risk and high-risk decisions.

The LLM serves as an explanation layer and is not permitted to override model predictions or make final business decisions.
